What makes it useful

Not a document scanner. A musical understanding layer.

Every capability is designed around faithful reconstruction first, because practice feedback is only trustworthy when the underlying score is correct.

Core product promise

Your teacher’s score becomes the shared reference.

The photo, corrected structured score, interactive rendering, and later performance feedback all point to the same musical events—not separate files that drift apart.

Faithful notation

More than pitch and duration

Score IR represents chords, beams, displayed accidentals, fingering, slurs, bowing, tuplets, dynamics, and multiple voices.

Correction first

Questions worth asking

What exists now—and what does not?

Is this already a downloadable product?
Not yet. The recognition engine, deterministic score representation, MusicXML export, and rendering tests exist. The correction interface and student practice experience are product milestones still to build.
What kind of sheet music is supported first?
The recognition model currently targets printed Western notation on a single treble staff, mostly monophonic melody, using the fixed V1 vocabulary. Richer notation is represented in Score IR but is not yet part of neural recognition training.
Why not have the AI generate MusicXML directly?
MusicXML is verbose and easy to make inconsistent. The model predicts a compact musical structure; deterministic software validates it, creates Score IR, and writes standards-compliant MusicXML.
How is accuracy being measured?
The project reports token, pitch, and duration error rates; exact systems; event and header accuracy; syntax and rhythm validity; and independent real-photo benchmarks. Sealed final sets are not reused for tuning.
Will it replace a music teacher?
No. The product is designed to carry the teacher’s exact assignment into the days between lessons. It should make practice more focused and observable, not decide musical interpretation on its own.

The long-term promise

From the page on the stand to feedback that understands the music.

The next proof is disciplined: finish M9.4, preserve Score IR’s representation boundary, then build the correction and practice surfaces on evidence—not assumptions.
