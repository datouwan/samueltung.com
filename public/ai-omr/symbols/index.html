Model design

From visual glyph to structured event

Recognition principles

What matters for OMR

Correct music depends on relationships—not just classifying isolated glyphs.

Geometry carries pitch

A notehead's vertical position relative to staff lines and clef determines pitch. Staff detection is therefore foundational.

Context resolves ambiguity

A curved line can be a tie or slur; a dot can be augmentation or articulation. Neighboring notes and rhythmic grammar decide the meaning.

Measures provide validation

Time signature and event durations create hard constraints. Impossible beat totals should trigger review or constrained decoding.

Scope note. “All music symbols” is effectively unbounded: SMuFL contains thousands of glyphs across Western, historical, percussion, microtonal, and specialist notation. This page intentionally defines the common student-practice taxonomy first and can grow with model coverage.
