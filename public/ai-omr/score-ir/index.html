Foundation milestone · complete

The violin acceptance example round-trips

A rights-clean dotted-eighth + sixteenth fixture proves double stops, stacked fingerings, a displayed sharp, explicit beams, MusicXML output, and visible Verovio rendering.

Chord / double stop

One event carries multiple simultaneous notes with deterministic chord serialization.

Explicit beam groups

Beam level and begin / continue / end / hook relationships survive round trip.

Displayed accidentals

Sounding pitch alteration and the visibly printed accidental remain separate.

Per-note fingering

Finger numbers remain attached to the correct note inside a chord.

Event(
  duration = 3/16,
  notes = [
    Note(B4, fingering=4),
    Note(G4, fingering=2)

  beams = [Beam(1, "begin")]

Event(
  duration = 1/16,
  notes = [Note(E4)],

    Beam(2, "forward_hook")
  ]

Sequencing rule: deterministic Score IR and rendering may advance beside Track A, but the recognizer stays on the fixed 383-token V1 target until the independent M9.4 real-photo gate is complete. Representation progress does not authorize a training-vocabulary change.
