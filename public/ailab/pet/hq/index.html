02 · Roadmap

Phases and their gates

Startup discipline for a hardware pet: every phase has a kill-or-continue gate, and each phase is roughly 10× cheaper than the one after it. Fail early, fail cheap.

  • Concept dossier done · Jul 2026 Species design, market study, component architecture — the three sibling pages of this hub. Gate passed: a coherent, differentiated concept with defensible unit economics on paper.
  • Phase 0/1 — Home works-like prototype next The wizard-of-Oz puppet is dropped (a Moflin at home already sets the alive-ness bar); Phases 0–1 merge into a staged home build — an ESP32-S3 body with the laptop as brain, foam body first, $305–609 all-in after the 2026-09-04 re-plan. Stage A presence blob ($144–272) → A′ shell print after the gate (+$40–90) → Stage B comprehension (+$0–20) → Stage C glide (+$109–207); active warmth stays an A+ drop-in (+$12–20). Full plan on the Proto page; electronics are cleared to order today, and CAD is off the critical path. Gate: the frond language reads without explanation and a comprehension moment lands ("sulks on your suitcase") — judged by someone who isn't Samuel, with the Moflin in the room.
  • Phase 2 — Looks-like alphas in homes A handful of hand-built units living with real families for 90+ days. Gate: retention past month 3 — the metric that killed the category graveyard (Aibo, Jibo, Vector, Cozmo).
  • Phase 3 — EVT / DVT / certification Tooling, molds, FCC/CE. The ~$2–4 M NRE check gets written here and nowhere earlier. Gate: landed COGS ≤ $260 at 20k/yr volume; safety and privacy architecture audited.
  • Phase 4 — D2C launch Direct-to-consumer first (the Lovot playbook), subscription attached from day one. Gate: the north star — 30–40k units/yr or >40% attach = a profitable company.

Dossier re-audited against the live site: six cross-page drifts fixed (HQ Stage A price, Proto Stage B price and stale safety text, battery 5,000 → 4,000 mAh with the roaming claim re-stated as 4–5 h, Lovot price ~$3,000 on both pages, Concept §09 path rewritten to the home-build plan).   • 2026-08-05Active warmth deferred to Stage A+. The Pi 5 dissipates 10–12 W under Whisper, the same order as the heater film, so the cooler’s exhaust ducts to a belly plate instead. Removes the build’s only dangerous subsystem plus the ADS1115, MOSFET, NTC and fuse; peak power ~50 W → ~28 W. Stage A $271–487 → $253–457, all-in unchanged at $424–750. Cost: Pi heat tracks CPU load, not mood, so Stage A tests warmth as baseline, not expression.
  • 2026-08-05Pre-flight pass before ordering — five would-have-failed findings. Three on critical path: WS2812B does not drive on a Pi 5 (the RP1 controller broke the PWM/DMA path) and glow is the frond channel, so it became APA102/SK9822 over SPI; the heater specified an NTC but the Pi has no ADC, leaving the thermal loop with no feedback at all, so an ADS1115 was added; and the mic HAT and MAX98357 both wanted the single I²S bus, so the amp was dropped as redundant. Plus a Pi 5 active cooler (Whisper pegs four cores under fur) and the Pi 5 camera cable, without which the Stage B camera will not physically plug in. Power budget peaks ~50 W, so one 27 W brick does not close it and a stationary Stage A moves to mains. Stage A $247–429 → $271–487, all-in $397–686 → $424–750 — half the servo upgrade, half parts the BOM was simply missing. Reasoning in the yura repo's PREFLIGHT.md.
  • 2026-08-05Frond actuation settled for Stage A: root-mounted sub-micro gimbals. Prompted by asking how two MG90S would fit in the head — they wouldn't, comfortably. Sub-micro (4–5 g) is ~¼ the volume and mass at the highest point of a 680 g creature, and quieter, which the aliveness gate cares about. A static-frond Stage A with posture deferred to a Stage A2 A/B test was explored and reverted the same day; posture stays in Stage A. Tendon drive is the likely production answer and is now a tracked decision.
