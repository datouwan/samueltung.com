Learn where to cut.
Let the solver flatten.

autouv is an automatic UV unwrapper for 3D models. A neural network predicts the seams an artist would place; classical parameterization and packing do the rest. Training labels come for free from the hundreds of thousands of meshes that already ship with artist-made UVs.

Thesis

Only one of the three steps needs learning.

Unwrapping is cutting, flattening, and packing. Two of those are solved by deterministic geometry. The third is a discrete, taste-driven decision, and that is exactly where automatic tools fall short of an artist.

Learned

Seam placement

Which edges to cut so charts are few, low-distortion, and follow the shape the way a human would. Combinatorial, and artists disagree with every heuristic in xatlas and Blender.

Solver

Flattening

Given a chart that is a topological disk, LSCM, ARAP, SLIM, and BFF flatten it with near-optimal distortion. No reason to learn coordinates.

Solver

Packing

Placing charts in the atlas is a bin-packing problem with good existing tools. xatlas packs charts once seams are decided.

Prior evidence

SeamGPT (2025) generates seam polylines with an autoregressive transformer and beats xatlas in user studies. PartUV (2025) drives charts from learned part segmentation. Nuvo (2023) learned UV maps end to end and produced artist-unfriendly charts, which is why the split matters.

Labels are free

Merge UV-split vertices by position, then an edge is a seam when its two faces disagree about the UV coordinates of its endpoints. Chart ids follow as connected components across non-seam edges. No human labeling.

Why now

Objaverse exposes roughly 800k licensed Sketchfab objects as GLB with per-object licenses. Mesh networks and point transformers are mature. A 10 to 50M parameter seam model trains in a day on one GPU.

Cut graph cleanup turns noisy per-edge probabilities into a valid set of cuts: threshold, join nearby segments, prune dangling stubs, add cuts until every chart is a topological disk under a distortion budget. Seams are transferred back to the original high-poly mesh by nearest edge.

License filter

Keep CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA. Drop CC-BY-NC and CC-BY-ND for anything that ships commercially. License, author, and source URL are stored with every sample.

Quality filter

  • 500 to 100k faces, near-manifold
  • Seam ratio in a plausible band: rejects untouched meshes and per-face shreds
  • Area-weighted conformal distortion: median under 1.5, p90 under 3
  • Few flipped or degenerate UV triangles
  • UVs inside the unit square: rejects tiled textures
  • Chart overlap under 5%: rejects mirrored layouts in v1

Sample format

One compressed npz per mesh: vertices, faces, UVs, geometric vertex ids, edge list, per-edge seam label, per-face chart id. A JSON sidecar holds metrics, reject reasons, and provenance.

Model

Three versions, each one earns the next.

Start with the cheapest thing that can be verified against artist seams. Upgrade only where measurements say the simpler version plateaus.

v1

Per-edge seam classifier

  • Inputs: positions, normals, curvature, dihedral angle, geodesic or heat-kernel features
  • Backbone: DiffusionNet, or a point transformer over sampled surface points projected to edges
  • Output: seam probability per edge
  • Loss: weighted BCE plus dice or focal; seams are 2 to 5% of edges
  • 10 to 50M parameters, one to two days on a single A100 or 4090
v2

Self-supervised refinement

Fine-tune from the v1 policy with a reward built from distortion, seam length, and chart count. No labels needed, so this gets past the inconsistency of artist data and lets the model exceed the average artist rather than imitate them.

v3

Autoregressive seam generation

If per-edge prediction plateaus on global coherence, move to a SeamGPT-style transformer that emits seam polylines token by token, conditioned on a shape encoder. More expensive, better long-range consistency.

Metrics

Every unwrap is scored the same way, artist or machine.

Baselines are xatlas and Blender Smart UV on the same meshes. Without the metric suite there is no way to tell whether the model is winning.

MetricWhat it measures
Seam F1, boundary IoUAgreement with artist seams, on held-out meshes only.
Conformal distortions1 / s2 of the per-face Jacobian, area-weighted median and p90. 1 is angle preserving.
Area distortions1 · s2 after removing global scale. 1 is area preserving.
L2 stretchSander stretch, sqrt((s1² + s2²) / 2), area-weighted mean.
Chart count, seam length ratioFewer charts and shorter seams mean fewer texture discontinuities.
Coverage, overlap, flippedAtlas utilization, fraction of overlapping pixels, fraction of inverted triangles.

Roadmap

Phases

Status is updated here as phases close. The repo holds the detailed plan.

PHASE 0

Baseline and metrics

In progress. Mesh loader that preserves artist UVs, seam and chart extraction, distortion and atlas metrics, quality filters, xatlas baseline, unit tests. Repo created 2026-09-01.

PHASE 1

Dataset

Objaverse harvest on a RunPod CPU pod against a network volume. License and quality filters, compact npz samples. Target: 20k curated meshes plus Amazon Berkeley Objects for validation.

PHASE 2

Seam classifier

v1 model, training loop, evaluation against artist seams and xatlas on held-out meshes.

PHASE 3

Cut graph cleanup, flatten, pack

End-to-end unwrap from a raw mesh. Seam transfer back to the original resolution.

PHASE 4

Self-supervised refinement

Distortion, seam length, and chart count as reward. Compare against v1 and artists in a small user study.

PHASE 5

Deployment

RunPod serverless endpoint: glTF or OBJ in, mesh with UVs out. Blender and Godot importer hooks for GameLoop.

Risks

Known ways this goes wrong.

Artist styles conflict

Hard-surface and organic assets get unwrapped differently. Either condition on an asset category or accept a blended style.

Mirrored UVs

Character artists overlap symmetric halves on purpose. A naive extractor treats them as normal seams. Excluded in v1, possible separate label class later.

Bad topology

Non-manifold and quad meshes break most mesh networks. Triangulate and repair in preprocessing; reject what cannot be repaired.

The model is not the product

A seam classifier alone never unwraps anything. Budget as much time for cut graph cleanup and solver integration as for training.
