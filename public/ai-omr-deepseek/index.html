What the measurements say

Four findings from two sealed benchmarks.

Both tracks practiced the same discipline — frozen manifests, sealed benchmarks, predeclared gates, fail-closed audits. The numbers can be trusted, which is what makes the trend informative.

1 · Representation beats capacity

Graphic-first's 6.1M-parameter CRNN out-scored Semantic's pretrained-Swin Transformer by 4× on identical real photos, trained ~25 minutes on purely synthetic renders. The difference is the task, not the model.

2 · Deterministic semantics wins

97.4% altered-note recovery and 95.0% zero-correction measures are properties of Graphic-first's engraving machine — code, not behavior. Detector-first inherits that property unchanged.

3 · The frontier is scope + deployment

Single-staff V1 recognition is closed (B1 sealed, all gates passed). Grand staff, full pages, and on-device latency are the open problems — and sequence models carry structural handicaps on both.

4 · OSS is a benchmark, not an engine

oemer's 2/5 exact with 60% conversion reliability and minute-scale CPU latency confirms the detector family is right while ruling it out as a product dependency. It becomes a benchmark column and a teacher.

Staged pipeline · PlayScore-style

Detector first. Assembly second.

The commercial leader (ReadScoreLib) is a staged, compiler-like detector pipeline — the same architecture. Perception produces geometry; code produces meaning.

Capture & rectify

VisionKit document scanner (perspective + deskew built in) or a live session with our own quad rectification. Adaptive thresholding for lighting and shadow.

Staff geometry

Locate systems, the five lines of each staff, and staff spacing with deterministic projections and priors — audited on synthetic degraded prints before any photo, like Graphic-first's splitter.

Symbol detection

One-stage detector over the full page: ~35 classes with boxes, staff assignment, vertical position, and confidence. Anchor-free heads for dense small glyphs.

Assembly

Noteheads → chords by overlap and stem; stems/beams/flags → durations; dots, rests, barlines → measures with beat repair; voices by stem direction. Pure code, property-tested.

Engraving state

Clef/key/time headers drive the engraving machine: key defaults, measure-scoped accidentals, ties carrying pitch — the Graphic-first property, extended to two staves.

Score IR → MusicXML

A deterministic relational Score IR (voices, dynamics, tuplets) emits canonical MusicXML with explicit accidentals, so the render always matches the ink. Rendered in-app with OSMD or Verovio.

Operating principles

Accuracy has a provenance.

01Rights-clean by construction

Public-domain or permissively licensed symbolic scores, self-rendered images, provenance preserved end to end.

02Deterministic where possible

The model recognizes ink. Explicit code validates rhythm, spells pitch, and generates MusicXML.

03Sealed benchmarks, one shot

Consumed benchmarks never become training data. Frozen protocols, digest integrity, predeclared gates.

04Measure real decoding

Token error rate, exact systems, altered-note recovery, zero-correction measures — never loss alone.
