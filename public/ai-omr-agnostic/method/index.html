现用读法：12 个小模型接力

现在读一页乐谱，不是一个模型读完，而是 12 个同样大小的小模型各读一遍整页，每一种记号取自最可靠的那一个（或那一对）。这一页记下它们各自负责什么、为什么会变成 12 个、要花多少时间，以及接下来把它们合并的想法。网页以后再整理，这里先存档。

METHOD RECORD · 2026-11-10

The working read: a relay of 12 small models

A page of music is not read by one model today. Twelve small models of the same size each read the whole page, and every kind of mark is taken from the model (or the pair) that reads it most reliably. This page records what each one does, why there are twelve, what it costs in time, and the plan to merge them. The site will be tidied later; this is the archive copy.

12 个模型各自负责什么

12 个都是同一种网络（卷积 + 双向 LSTM，每个约 31 MB），差别只在训练时教它写下哪些记号。

另外还有一个外部的开源读谱程序做第二次对照，只用来标出可疑的小节，不参与训练，也不会放进产品。

为什么会变成 12 个

  • 一个模型学了新记号，音符就读差。连续四轮都量到同样的结果：把新记号教给音符模型，合成数据上分数变高，真实照片上音符变差。所以音符模型固定不动，每一类新记号另外训练一个模型，再接到音符上。
  • 单独一个记号模型会「编」记号。例如指法：单个模型找得到一半多，但会写错几十个数字；要求两个模型都读到同一个数字才采用，写错的几乎都消失。准确是靠「两个一致」换来的，所以每一类记号都是一对。
  • 只加不减。每一次改进只在作者修过的页面上确实变好才采用，旧的来源在新来源没有更好的地方就留着，于是越接越长。

要花多少时间

  • 电脑显卡：每个模型读一页 0.6 秒，12 个约 7 秒；只用处理器约 74 秒。
  • iPad Pro M4（浏览器实测，只测音符模型）：每页 3.4 秒；12 个全跑估计约 41 秒，iPhone 16 Pro 估计约 86 秒。
  • 如果变成「音符模型 + 合并后的记号模型」：iPad 估计约 7 秒（2 个模型）到 10 秒（3 个模型）。

产品是 iOS 应用，模型必须少、必须小，这就是下面合并的原因。

合并的想法（第 11 轮）

  1. 音符模型不动。1 号照旧，合并前后音符必须一模一样。
  2. 3–12 号合成一个「记号模型」。一次读出符杠、跳音／重音／保持音、指法、弓法、延长号、装饰音、琶音、连线。它也会读音符（记号要挂在音符上），但它的音符不印出来。
  3. 从 10–11 号接着训练。它们已经会大部分记号，只差连线的形状和更多的符杠写法。
  4. 语料合在一起，而且每一行都标全所有记号。以前连线的语料没有标符杠和指法，符杠的语料没有标连线形状；缺标注的行会教模型「这里没有」，所以缺的部分要重新生成，不能直接混在一起。
  5. 先训练两个（因为现在的准确靠两个一致），云端训练、本机评分，预计花费约 70 美元。
  6. 训练前先做一次不花钱的测量：只用现有的 10–11 号做接力，和现用读法在作者修过的页面上逐类比较，定下合并后必须达到的数字。

训练后要量的，不先假设：一个模型加上把握度门槛能不能代替「两个一致」；合并模型自己读的音符能不能代替 2 号做对照；每一类记号找到多少、写错多少；把它多读出来的记号裁图出来看。

风险：连线（长连线、把延音线读成连线）和符杠是最难的两类；十类记号挤进一个 31 MB 的模型可能装不下。哪一类输了，就先留在原来的模型上——结果可能先是「12 个变 4 个」，再到「12 个变 3 个」甚至 2 个。

What each of the 12 models does

All twelve are the same network (convolutions + a bidirectional LSTM, about 31 MB each). They differ only in which marks they were taught to write down.

One external open-source reader also runs as a second opinion. It only flags doubtful bars; it is never trained on and will not ship in the product.

Why there are twelve

  • Teaching one model new marks makes it read notes worse. Four rounds in a row measured the same thing: add new marks to the note model and the synthetic score goes up while the notes on real photos get worse. So the note model stays fixed, and each new family of marks gets its own model, attached to the notes afterwards.
  • A single mark model invents marks. Fingering is the example: one model finds more than half but writes dozens of wrong digits; requiring two models to read the same digit removes almost all of them. Precision is bought with "two agree", so every family of marks is a pair.
  • Add, never remove. A change is adopted only when it is measurably better on the pages the author has corrected, and an older source stays wherever the new one is not better. The relay only grows.

What it costs in time

  • Desktop GPU: 0.6 s per model per page, about 7 s for all twelve; about 74 s on CPU only.
  • iPad Pro M4 (measured in the browser, note model only): 3.4 s per page; all twelve are estimated at about 41 s, and about 86 s on an iPhone 16 Pro.
  • With "note model + merged mark model": an estimated 7 s on iPad (2 models) to 10 s (3 models).

The product is an iOS app, so the models have to be few and small. That is the reason for the merge below.

The merge plan (round 11)

  1. The note model is untouched. Model 1 stays as it is; the notes must be identical before and after the merge.
  2. Models 3–12 become one "mark model". One pass reads beams, staccato / accent / tenuto, fingering, bowing, fermata, ornaments, arpeggio and slurs. It also reads notes (marks hang on notes), but its notes are not printed.
  3. Training continues from models 10–11. They already know most marks and lack only slur shapes and more beam patterns.
  4. One combined corpus in which every line carries every label. The slur corpus had no beam or fingering labels and the beam corpus had no slur shapes; a line with missing labels teaches "nothing here", so the missing parts are regenerated rather than mixed as they are.
  5. Two seeds first (today's precision depends on two agreeing), trained in the cloud and scored locally, at an estimated cost of about 70 US dollars.
  6. A free measurement before training: build a relay from models 10–11 alone, compare it family by family with the working read on the author's corrected pages, and fix the numbers the merged model must reach.

To be measured after training, not assumed: whether one model with a confidence threshold can replace "two agree"; whether the merged model's own notes can replace model 2 as the cross-check; how many marks of each family are found and how many are wrong; and crops of every mark it adds, looked at by eye.

Risks: slurs (long slurs, ties read as slurs) and beams are the two hardest families, and ten families may not fit in one 31 MB model. A family that loses stays on its current model for now — the outcome may be "12 become 4" first, then 3 or even 2.
