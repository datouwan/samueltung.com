Parallel research track · founded 2026-08-13

Recognize what is printed.
Derive what it means.

A from-scratch parallel bet on the same goal as the main AI-OMR effort: reliable single-staff recognition from ordinary phone photos. The difference is the factorization — the neural network reads glyphs at staff positions, and a deterministic engraving state machine turns them into music. Pitch spelling is code, not learned behavior.

并行研究路线 · 2026-08-13 开始

认出纸上印了什么，
再推出它是什么音。

和主线 AI-OMR 目标相同、从零开始的另一条路：用普通手机照片可靠地读出乐谱。差别在分工——神经网络只读「哪个符号印在五线谱的哪个位置」，再由一套确定的记谱规则程序把它们变成音乐。音高怎么拼写是代码，不是学出来的行为。

Why this track exists

The main track's own measurements point at the representation.

Track A is a semantic image-to-token transformer with excellent experimental discipline. Its numbers are trustworthy — and they show a structural ceiling, not a tuning problem.

695 / 733key-default pitch spellings correct on synthetic images when the key header is right — the model can do key arithmetic in-domain
0 / 70the same measurement on real photos, even with 81/100 key headers correct — the learned join collapses under domain shift
87.4%of altered reference notes are emitted as naturals on real photos — the dominant failure class by far

Loss weighting failed

2× altered-token loss raised synthetic altered emission to ~20% but regressed real-photo error. Forcing the class prior does not transfer.

Post-hoc fixes are unsafe

Applying the key signature to naturals after decoding corrupts legitimate explicit naturals — thousands of them exist. Once the decoder discards the printed/derived distinction, no code can recover it.

Gains are asymptoting

Every well-run intervention — key-aware corpora, calibrated augmentation, encoder adaptation — buys single-digit relative improvements. Real-photo TER sits at ~0.26 vs ~0.07 clean, with 0/60 exact systems.

为什么有这条路线

主线自己量到的数字，指向的是表示方式。

A 路线是一个从图像直接输出「语义记号」的 Transformer，实验做得很严谨。它的数字可信——而这些数字显示的是结构上的天花板，不是调参能解决的。

695 / 733在合成图像上，调号读对时，按调号默认升降的音高拼写正确——模型在训练分布内会做调号运算
0 / 70同一项测量放到真实照片上，即使 100 个调号读对 81 个——学出来的这一步在真实照片上完全失效
87.4%真实照片上，本该升降的音被输出成还原音的比例——远超其他错误类型

加重损失没有用

把升降音的损失加到 2 倍，合成数据上升降音输出升到约 20%，真实照片的错误率反而变差。硬推类别比例不会迁移。

事后修补不安全

解码之后再把调号套到还原音上，会破坏本来就印着的还原号——这样的音有几千个。解码器一旦丢掉「印出来的」和「推出来的」的区别，任何代码都找不回来。

进步越来越小

每一项做得很好的改进——带调号的语料、校准过的增强、编码器适配——都只带来个位数百分比的相对改善。真实照片的记号错误率停在约 0.26（干净图约 0.07），60 行里没有一行全对。

Theory

Split the problem where the evidence says it breaks.

Reading music is two different jobs. Seeing ink is perception. Knowing what the ink means is arithmetic. Only one of them needs a neural network.

The model's output vocabulary is agnostic: 475 tokens naming printed symbols and their vertical staff positions — notehead shapes, printed accidentals, rests, dots, barlines, ties. Nothing in it encodes sounding pitch, key arithmetic, or measure state. A model trained on this vocabulary cannot exhibit the naturalization defect, because spelling is not its job. Even the key signature is just a run of ordinary accidental glyphs — reading a four-sharp signature becomes four local detections instead of one fragile 15-way classification.

clef.G2acc.flat@4acc.flat@7acc.flat@3 time.2/4note.sixteenth@4rest.eighth acc.natural@4note.quarter@4barline

A real target line: three flats, 2/4, a sixteenth on the middle line (B♭ by key — the converter knows, the model doesn't have to), an eighth rest, then an explicit natural. The deterministic converter applies key defaults, measure-scoped accidental memory, and tie carry-over — the standard engraving rules, implemented once, tested exhaustively, identical in both directions.

模型输出的词表是「只看图形」的：475 个记号，写的是印出来的符号和它在五线谱上的高低位置——符头形状、印出来的变音记号、休止符、附点、小节线、延音线。里面没有任何东西表示实际音高、调号运算或小节内的状态。用这个词表训练的模型不可能出现「升降音变还原音」的毛病，因为拼写不归它管。连调号也只是一串普通的变音记号——读四个升号的调号，变成四次局部识别，而不是一次脆弱的 15 选 1。

clef.G2acc.flat@4acc.flat@7acc.flat@3 time.2/4note.sixteenth@4rest.eighth acc.natural@4note.quarter@4barline

一行真实的训练目标：三个降号，2/4 拍，第三线上一个十六分音符（按调号是降 B——转换程序知道，模型不需要知道），一个八分休止符，然后一个印出来的还原号。确定的转换程序负责套用调号默认值、小节内变音记号的记忆、延音线的延续——标准的记谱规则，只实现一次、彻底测试、正反两个方向完全一致。

Method

Four deliberate departures from the main track.

Each one targets a measured failure mode, not a hunch.

1 · Agnostic targets, deterministic semantics

The engraving state machine converts semantic scores to printed-symbol sequences and back, fail-closed in both directions, with round-trip identity proven over randomized sweeps. It is also the ground-truth generator: every rendered system gets exact graphical labels with zero manual annotation — and the MusicXML writer takes its printed accidentals from the same function, so the rendered image and the training target can never disagree.

2 · CTC instead of autoregression

A music staff reads monotonically left to right, and CTC alignment bakes that in as a hard prior. The recognizer physically cannot repeat n-grams, skip around the staff, or fail to terminate — the entire grammar-masking / repetition-blocking / EOS-bias scaffold of the autoregressive approach becomes unnecessary. The model is a 6M-parameter CRNN, ~5× smaller than the main track's encoder alone.

3 · Staff-interline normalization

Every image — clean render, degraded render, real photo — passes one deterministic pipeline: estimate the staff interline from ink-run periods, rescale so the interline is exactly 16 px, center the staff on a fixed 192 px canvas. Position classes then map to stable pixel offsets. Verified on all 180 real training photos without a learned component.

4 · Real photos are evaluation-only

The opposite of the main track's current cycle, on purpose. A measured degradation pipeline (perspective, paper bow, illumination, shadows, gray ink, sensor noise, JPEG) must carry the domain by itself: its output statistics are gated to fall inside the real photos' interquartile windows for brightness, contrast, and sharpness. The gate passed on 2026-08-13. Real photos stay unconsumed for honest measurement.

设计

和主线刻意不同的四个地方。

每一个都针对一种量到的失败，而不是凭感觉。

1 · 目标只写图形，语义由规则决定

记谱规则状态机把乐谱转成「印出来的符号序列」，也能转回来；两个方向遇到不合规则的情况都直接报错，并用随机扫描证明来回完全一致。它同时是标准答案的生成器：每一行渲染出来的谱都自动得到精确的图形标注，不需要人工标注——MusicXML 写出程序用的也是同一个函数来决定印哪些变音记号，所以渲染出来的图和训练目标永远不会对不上。

2 · 用 CTC，不用自回归

五线谱是从左到右单调读下去的，CTC 对齐把这一点变成硬性前提。识别器在机制上就不可能重复一段、在谱上乱跳、或停不下来——自回归方法需要的语法屏蔽、重复阻挡、结束符偏置这一整套都用不着。模型是一个 600 万参数的 CRNN，比主线单是编码器还小约 5 倍。

3 · 按谱线间距归一化

每一张图——干净渲染、退化渲染、真实照片——都走同一条确定的流程：从墨迹的周期估出谱线间距，缩放到间距正好 16 像素，把谱表放在固定 192 像素高的画布中央。这样每个高低位置对应稳定的像素偏移。在全部 180 张真实训练照片上验证过，没有用到任何学习的组件。

4 · 真实照片只用来评估

和主线目前的做法刻意相反。一条量过的退化流程（透视、纸张弯曲、光照、阴影、灰墨、传感器噪点、JPEG）必须独自承担「像照片」这件事：它输出的亮度、对比度、清晰度统计要落在真实照片的四分位区间内。这一关在 2026-08-13 通过。真实照片不进训练，留作诚实的测量。

Milestones with gates, not vibes.

Every milestone exits through a predeclared gate. Filled dots are sealed results; the highlighted dot is where the work is right now.

A0–A1

Representation core & data pipeline

475-token agnostic vocabulary, the fail-closed engraving state machine (pitch spelling as tested code), canonical MusicXML writer/reader, key-aware generator, and an audited corpus builder — round-trip identity proven everywhere.

A2–A3

Normalization, degradation & the CTC recognizer

Interline-normalized canvas, camera-calibrated degradation gated against real-photo statistics, and a 6M-parameter CRNN+CTC: TER 0.003, 93% exact on degraded synthetic validation after ~25 minutes of training.

A4–A5

Real-photo head-to-head: the bet won

Same 60 held-out photos, identical metric: TER 0.0632 vs Track A's 0.2561, 29/60 exact vs 0/60, every printable accidental recovered. Graphic-first becomes the recommended backbone.

A6

Distractor ink & the accidental fix

Slurs, lyrics, dynamics and tempo text rendered as clutter with targets untouched; an injector makes capture pages print their chromatic accidentals — the first prints whose ink matches their labels.

B1

Sealed benchmark: all eight gates passed

120 never-photographed systems, checkpoint declared by SHA-256 before capture, evaluated exactly once: TER 0.0190, 109/120 exact, 97.4% accidental recovery, 95% of measures correction-free. V1 single-staff development closed.

A7

Score IR v2: chords — validated on real photos

One grouping token, per-notehead accidentals through the same engraving state machine, retrained on an 11k-system corpus. The C1 real-photo development set (120 fresh systems, 90 chordful) confirms it: TER 0.0130, 100/120 exact, 99.3% of chord events with correct structure — chordful real-photo error lands below the sealed monophonic benchmark.

A8

Full-page multi-system reading — core gates passed

A page photo now reads with no page map at all: staff detection that survives keystone, paper curl, carpet backgrounds and defocus (strip-wise line extraction, staves as evenly-spaced five-line progressions), validated on the C1 photos (120/120), a 270-system variable-layout synthetic corpus (270/270), and end to end through the recognizer: map-blind TER 0.0162 vs 0.0130 with known geometry, zero mis-attributed systems. The page detector then went through four more sealed real-photo rounds — see P3.5 below.

P1

Bass clef on a single staff

The plan bent toward the end goal — piano grand staff, then violin + piano — so the next step after full pages was the second clef. Checkpoint p1-s2: C1 TER 0.0091, 104/120 exact, the Ode read perfectly, method book 6/9 with repeats read. Checkpoint p3f-s2: C1 0.0032 / 111 exact, method book 8/10, synthetic grand end-to-end 16/16 pages · TER 0.0039 · 20/20 assembled, Ferrante Lesson 68 (a real piano page) 3/3 grand systems assembled. Three follow-up campaigns (p3g/p3h/p3i) were not selected: corpus knobs shift the distribution — each bought a synthetic gain and one real-page skill at the cost of another.

P3.5

Sealed real-photo grand-staff benchmark — passed on the fifth package

B1's discipline, on piano pages: 60 printed grand systems on 20 pages, checkpoint pinned by SHA-256 before capture, photographed on a desk and a carpet, evaluated exactly once, photos consumed. Gates: structure ≥ 58/60, staff TER ≤ 0.015, assembly ≥ 54/60. G1 57/60 · 0.135 · 48/60. G2 57/60 · 0.082 · 54/60. G3 60/60 · 0.021 · 56/60. G4 57/60 · 0.127 · 54/60. Every failure was the page detector, never the recognizer: a strip of carpet at the page edge, a bright wedge cut along a side-light gradient, a hard cast shadow, a 14-px staff and a beam-saturated one. Each was diagnosed on the sealed run, reproduced and fixed on an unsealed capture, and left the sealed number standing. P3.8

Recognizer capacity campaigns — new checkpoint of record, sealed on G6

G5's residue was dots, ties and accidentals. p3j (the same recipe, corpus doubled, 40 epochs, no knobs) beat the record on every sealed probe and collapsed on the photographed method book: fingering digits and dashed position brackets read as 8va. p3k rendered that book clutter into the generator as ink only; all three seeds kept the gains and restored the book. p3k-s3 was pinned and put through a fresh sealed package: G6 — structure 60/60, staff TER 0.0084, 60/60 assembled, 105/120 exact, half the pages on a dark cloth under a studio panel, a background no sealed run had seen. It is the checkpoint of record. Live reads, photo next to render, on the progress page.

P4.1

Chord symbols — recognized, not ignored (campaigns closed)

The songbook pages in the live probe carry chord symbols the reader had no word for. Decision: recognize them. Score IR gained a chord symbol per event (root, accidental, 14 kinds, slash bass), 30 append-only tokens, MusicXML <harmony> in and out, and a generator that prints them the way songbooks do. The first campaign was stopped after fifteen minutes: the symbols were off the recognizer's canvas — engraving aligns them to the highest ink of the line and stacks them above tempo words, so a quarter of them fell outside the four interlines of headroom the reader had always seen. The canvas grew to ten interlines above the staff and now travels inside the checkpoint (older checkpoints keep theirs, byte-identical), the detector's crop grew with it, and the grand staff carries symbols on its upper staff. The first seed, p4b-s1, reads chord symbols off a real songbook photo — half of the printed symbols exactly, four in five roots — and beats the record on the grand-staff gate, but it also invents symbols from the titles, tempo words and lyrics the taller canvas now shows it, so it is not the checkpoint of record. Nine more campaigns followed (p4c–p4k: words-are-not-chords clutter, book symbol typography, slurs as targets on a deeper canvas, a songbook-layout corpus, dense harmony, a clutter ablation, clutter as a dose). Two invisible-target bugs were found and fixed on the way — distractor slur ink drawn over slur targets, slurs under low notes clipped off the canvas — and the trade-off was mapped: text clutter in training is what makes the reader drop real symbols (no clutter: 13 of 24 on the songbook page, but symbols invented from titles); the dose moved both numbers the right way without clearing the whole bar. Decision (2026-09-06): stop here, carry 5–11 of 24 as the state, and move to the read side. The reads are on the progress page.

P4.6

Record sealed on G7; songbook systems assembled on the read side

p4h-s1 — the seed trained on the songbook-layout corpus with slurs and the deep canvas — became the checkpoint of record on a re-read of the consumed G6 photos. That is not the project's standard, so a seventh package was built, the checkpoint pinned by SHA-256, twenty pages photographed and evaluated exactly once: G7 — structure 60/60, staff TER 0.0013, 116/120 staves exact, 60/60 assembled with zero repairs. Best sealed result the project has produced; all four misses are one dropped tie across a barline on a bass staff. The read side now assembles a treble vocal line found above a braced pair into one three-staff songbook system — chord symbols on the vocal line, repairs flagged per measure — and prints it; the same rule covers a violin part over piano. H1, a sealed package of forty such systems with book-style symbols and lyrics, was photographed and evaluated once the same day: not passed — one vocal line sat 14.7 interlines above its piano, over a 14-interline cap in the grouping rule, and the page rule cost both systems on that page (note TER 0.054 against a 0.015 gate; structure 38/40 and assembly 38/40 met). On the 38 systems the pipeline grouped: note TER 0.0003, 113/114 staves exact, and 112 of 112 chord symbols read, none invented, six symbol-free lines left clean — the first sealed symbol baseline, on the project's own engraving. The cap is fixed after the run; the sealed number stands, and H2 will be a fresh package. The commercial songbook still reads at 5–11 of 24: that gap is engraving realism — the book's faces and spacing — not photos.

P5.1

Dynamics and tempo text — read, printed, measured

The first item of the print-fidelity list. Thirty-six tokens for the dynamics (pp … ff, sfz, cresc., dim.), twelve tempo words and the metronome mark; the marks that had been ignore-ink since Phase 3 became targets, every rendered document is audited against its tokens, the new ink was measured by height band and staff before training, and the detector's crop below a staff now keeps the whole inter-staff gap so the piano's dynamics reach the right-hand line (the record checkpoint re-validated identical under the new crops). Three seeds overnight — one on the local 4090, two on a shared Nebius RTX PRO 6000 at 5.9 steps a second for $10.61. Dynamics are solved: 99.5% read, 0.2% spurious, and correct under the right measures on the photographed Bach and Handel pages. Tempo words read; metronome digits come back right about half the time (dropped or confused digits) — the next campaign's target. Notation held on the C1 gate (better than the record) and slipped on the hardest real pages, so p4h-s1 stays the checkpoint of record. Reads on the progress page.

P6

Tuplets, the sf family, and what a mid-piece piano page really prints

Triplets and sextuplets became targets (sounding versus printed durations, MusicXML time-modification, beaming by printed shape), the sf/sfp/fp/fz dynamics and the articulation marks as ignore-ink. Three seeds (two on Nebius for $13, one local): in distribution the tuplet work is finished — 440 of 440 groups read, none invented. The photographed piano page that motivated it did not move, and the photo says why: from measure 37 on it prints no tuplet numbers and no time signature — six beamed notes per group, fingerings the only digits. The transcriber knew the sextuplets from earlier pages; no single photo can. The same page exposed a training gap: every grand-staff crop we had ever rendered printed a meter, so on a meterless grand page each seed invented its own (0 to 10 of 10 systems). Continuation systems now run at 35% on the grand crops, and a fine-tune from the worst seed (p6b, $2.52) invents 0–1 — while the metronome digits jumped from 177 to 259 of 357 lines exact, the sign that 40 epochs had not converged. The violin page reads 58–61 of 71 measures across seeds, the piano page 2–4 of 24; two pages cannot rank seeds, and the record stays p4h-s1. Reads on the progress page.

P7

Hairpins and grace notes as targets; the longer campaign

Crescendo and diminuendo wedges and grace notes joined the vocabulary (559 → 649 tokens, append-only), with a dense 8va slice for exposure, and the first campaign trained 60 epochs instead of 40 — two seeds on the local 4090, no cloud. The extra epochs paid: validation kept improving to epoch 55–58, the metronome digits reached 315–319 of 357 lines exact (259 before). p7a-s2 is the working checkpoint: the sealed G7 package passes with 114 of 120 staves exact and all 60 systems clean, C1 reads 118 of 120, tuplets 460 of 460, grace notes 0.998 with none invented, and the live2 violin page reads 62 of 71 measures with nothing flagged — the best read of that page so far. The record stays p4h-s1 (116 on G7). Hairpins are the residue: in distribution 0.97–0.99, but on the photographed page the model sees the wedge open and never its tip — our renders end a hairpin at a note's onset, books end it in the air after the last note — so the next round moves the tip in the render and exposes it again. The evaluation also found two read-side faults that had been costing whole systems on real pages (a final double bar read as two barlines; a second unmatched slur end on a continuation line), both fixed. Done the same day: the render now ends 70% of hairpins in the air after the last covered note, and a fine-tune of p7a-s2 (p7b-s1, 15 epochs, 5 h local) learned it — dense-probe recall 0.85 → 0.97, two of the violin page's five hairpins read as complete pairs where before none were, every sealed number unchanged. p7b-s1 is the working checkpoint. Reads on the progress page.

P8

live3 ground truth, 32nd notes, and the read-side repairs that were worth more than a retrain

2026-09-16 to 09-19. Bar-length reasoning landed on the read side (unmarked sextuplets and a missing meter are decided from how the bars fill). Ten new phone pages of the three target layouts — piano, violin, violin + piano — got an eye-checked ground truth of 166 measures. It named the next gaps: a Handel Larghetto lost nine measures to 32nd notes, silently. 32nd notes became a target (vocabulary 693); the first fine-tune learned them (0.995) but moved the model's beam-count habit on real photos — eighths read as sixteenths, tremolo half notes as 32nds. Two answers, both kept: a second fine-tune with a 3% dose, tremolo slashes and thick photographed beams taught as ink (p8b), and — after counting why staves failed — read-side repairs: a chord read with two durations or an accidental one step off its notehead used to discard a whole staff; now it is repaired and flagged. Ground truth: live3 137 → 149 of 166 measures (90%), silent errors 15 → 9; live2 72 of 95; sealed G7 still passes (115 of 120 staves exact). p8b-s2 became the working checkpoint; the checkpoint of record stays p4h-s1. Update 09-19: a second ground truth (372 measures in all) showed that the Mozart pages were failing on something else entirely — filled noteheads that photograph patchy gray were read as half notes. The reader now looks at every staff twice (as shot, and through an ink curve) and keeps the read whose bars add up, and a fine-tune on mottled ink (p8c-s1, local, free) made the plain read right by itself: live3 293 of 372 (record 178), live2 73 of 95, and sealed G7 at 116 of 120 staves / 60 clean pages — the record's own numbers. p8c-s1 is the working checkpoint. Cloud note: this training is bound by CPU-side photo augmentation, so an RTX PRO 6000 and an H100 run it at the same speed — the cheaper card wins. Reads on the progress page.

A4–A5

真实照片上的正面对比：这一注押对了

同样 60 张没用过的照片、同一种评分：记号错误率 0.0632，对 A 路线的 0.2561；全对 29/60，对 0/60，所有印出来的变音记号都读回来了。「先读图形」成为建议的主干。

A6

干扰墨迹与变音记号的修正

连线、歌词、力度、速度文字作为干扰渲染出来，目标不变；并让拍摄用的谱页把半音变音记号真的印出来——第一批墨迹和标注完全一致的打印页。

B1

封存基准测试：八个关卡全部通过

120 行从没拍过的谱，拍摄前先用 SHA-256 声明检查点，只评估一次：记号错误率 0.0190，109/120 全对，变音记号找回 97.4%，95% 的小节不用修改。第一版单行谱的开发到此结束。

A7

和弦——在真实照片上验证

一个分组记号，每个符头的变音记号都走同一套规则状态机，在 1.1 万行的语料上重新训练。C1 真实照片开发集（120 行新谱，90 行有和弦）确认：记号错误率 0.0130，100/120 全对，99.3% 的和弦结构正确——有和弦的真实照片错误率比封存的单音基准还低。

A8

整页多行读谱——核心关卡通过

一张整页照片现在不需要任何版面信息就能读：找谱表的程序经得起梯形变形、纸张卷曲、地毯背景和失焦，在 C1 照片（120/120）和 270 行版式各异的合成语料（270/270）上验证，并且接上识别器端到端：不给版面的记号错误率 0.0162，对已知版面的 0.0130，没有一行归错。之后找谱表的程序又经过四轮封存的真实照片测试——见下面 P3.5。

P1

单行谱上的低音谱号

计划向最终目标——钢琴大谱表，再到小提琴＋钢琴——靠拢，所以整页之后下一步是第二种谱号。检查点 p1-s2：C1 记号错误率 0.0091，104/120 全对，《欢乐颂》全对，教本 6/9 并读出反复记号。在这里学到并一直沿用的选择规则：用综合标准（合成验证＋真实页面），绝不只看合成验证——每一次多种子训练里它都选错过。

P3.1–P3.4

大谱表：配对、组合、合成里程碑

两行同步的图形记号通过同一套规则状态机组合成钢琴的 MusicXML，宽容的修补会标出来等待检查；用穿过两行之间的小节线找出花括号连着的一对谱表；行中换谱号、8va 括线、和弦加入出题程序。检查点 p3f-s2：C1 0.0032／111 全对，教本 8/10，合成大谱表端到端 16/16 页，一张真实钢琴页 3/3 行组合成功。之后三轮（p3g/p3h/p3i）没有被选上：调语料的旋钮会移动分布——每次换来合成上的进步和一项真实页面的能力，却丢掉另一项。

P3.5

封存的真实照片大谱表基准——第五套通过

把 B1 的纪律用在钢琴页上：20 页 60 行打印的大谱表，拍摄前用 SHA-256 固定检查点，在桌面和地毯上拍，只评估一次，照片用过即作废。关卡：结构 ≥ 58/60，记号错误率 ≤ 0.015，组合 ≥ 54/60。G1 到 G4 都没过，G5 60/60 · 0.0121 · 58/60——通过。每一次失败都出在找谱表的程序，从来不是识别器：页边的一条地毯、侧光造成的亮楔形、硬阴影、太小的谱表、符杠太密的谱表。每一个都在封存结果上诊断、在没封存的照片上重现并修好，封存的数字保持原样。G1 到 G5 之间识别器没有变过。

P3.8

识别器容量的几轮训练——新的记录检查点，在 G6 上封存

G5 剩下的错误是附点、延音线和变音记号。p3j（同样的配方，语料加倍，40 轮）在每个封存测试上都超过记录，却在拍下来的教本上崩了：指法数字和虚线把位括线被读成 8va。p3k 把教本上的这些杂物只当墨迹渲染进出题程序；三个种子都保住了进步并把教本读回来。p3k-s3 被固定并通过一套全新的封存测试：G6——结构 60/60，记号错误率 0.0084，60/60 组合成功，105/120 全对，一半的页面拍在摄影灯下的深色布上，是以前封存测试没见过的背景。实拍对照见实拍进度。

P4.1

和弦标记——要读出来，不是忽略（已收尾）

歌本页面上有和弦标记，读谱程序原来没有对应的词。决定：读出来。词表只增不改地加了 30 个记号，MusicXML 读写都支持，出题程序按歌本的方式印出来。第一轮训练十五分钟就停了：这些标记在识别器的画布之外——它们印在一行最高的墨迹之上，四分之一落在读谱程序一直看到的范围外面。画布加高到谱表上方十个间距。第一个种子 p4b-s1 能从真实歌本照片上读出一半的和弦标记，但也会从标题、速度文字和歌词里「编」出标记来，所以不当作记录。之后又做了九轮（p4c–p4k），途中修了两个「目标看不见」的错误，也摸清了取舍：训练里的文字干扰让读谱程序丢掉真的标记（不加干扰：歌本页 24 个读对 13 个，但会从标题编出标记）。2026-09-06 决定到此为止，以 24 个读对 5–11 个为现状，转到读取端的工作。

P4.6

记录在 G7 上封存；歌本的三行谱在读取端组合

p4h-s1 成为记录检查点后，专门做了第七套封存测试，拍二十页、只评估一次：G7——结构 60/60，记号错误率 0.0013，116/120 行全对，60/60 组合成功、没有任何修补，是这个项目最好的封存结果。读取端现在能把花括号上方的一行人声谱和钢琴组合成三行一组的歌本谱——同样的规则也适用于小提琴＋钢琴。H1（四十组这样的三行谱）同一天拍摄并评估：没有通过——有一行人声谱离钢琴 14.7 个间距，超过分组规则的 14 间距上限，那一页的两组都丢了。在成功分组的 38 组上：记号错误率 0.0003，112 个和弦标记全部读对，没有编出来的。上限在测试后已修正；封存的数字保持原样。

P5.1

力度和速度文字——读出、印出、量过

「打印还原度」清单的第一项。力度记号（pp … ff、sfz、cresc.、dim.）、十二个速度词和节拍器标记共 36 个记号，从「只当墨迹」变成目标。三个种子一夜训练完。力度解决了：读出 99.5%，多读 0.2%，在拍下来的巴赫和亨德尔页面上都标在正确的小节下面。速度词能读；节拍器数字大约一半读对——留给下一轮。音符在最难的真实页面上略有退步，所以记录仍是 p4h-s1。

P6

连音、sf 一族，以及曲子中间的钢琴页到底印了什么

三连音和六连音成为目标，sf/sfp/fp/fz 和演奏记号当作墨迹。在训练分布内连音已经完成——440 组全部读出，没有编出来的。但促成这件事的那张钢琴页没有变好，照片说明了原因：从第 37 小节起它不印连音数字，也不印拍号——每组六个连在一起的音，页面上唯一的数字是指法。抄谱的人是从前几页知道那是六连音的；单看一张照片谁也不知道。这一页还暴露了训练的缺口：我们渲染过的每一行大谱表都印了拍号，所以遇到没有拍号的页面，每个种子都自己编一个。现在 35% 的大谱表是「接续行」（不印拍号），再训练之后编出来的降到 0–1 个。

P7

渐强渐弱记号和倚音成为目标；更长的训练

渐强、渐弱的开口记号和倚音加入词表（559 → 649 个记号，只增不改），并且第一次训练 60 轮而不是 40 轮——多出来的轮数有回报。p7a-s2 成为现用检查点：G7 以 114/120 行全对通过，C1 118/120，连音 460/460，倚音 0.998 且没有编出来的，live2 小提琴页 71 小节读对 62 小节。剩下的是渐强渐弱记号：真实页面上模型看得见开口，看不见尖端——我们的渲染让它结束在音符开始处，而书上是结束在最后一个音之后的空白处。当天改了渲染并接着训练（p7b-s1），密集测试的找回率从 0.85 升到 0.97，封存的数字都没变。

P8

live3 的标准答案、三十二分音符，以及比重新训练更值的读取端修补

2026-09-16 到 09-19。读取端加入按小节时值推理（没标的六连音和缺的拍号，从小节怎么填满来判断）。三种目标版式——钢琴、小提琴、小提琴＋钢琴——的十张新手机照片有了人工核对的标准答案（166 小节）。它指出下一个缺口：三十二分音符。把它加入目标后，模型学会了，却改变了它在真实照片上数符杠的习惯。两个办法都留下：用 3% 的剂量再训练（p8b），以及读取端修补——以前一个和弦读出两种时值、或变音记号偏一格，整行就被丢掉，现在修好并标出来。live3 137 → 166 小节读对 149（90%）。09-19 更新：第二份标准答案（一共 372 小节）显示莫扎特那几页错在别的地方——拍出来斑驳发灰的实心符头被读成二分音符。读谱程序现在每行看两遍（原图、调过墨色曲线的图），留下小节凑得齐的那一遍；再用斑驳墨迹训练（p8c-s1）后，live3 372 小节读对 293。p8c-s1 成为现用检查点。
